### **Assistant Professor**  
**July 2021 – Present**  

As an Assistant Professor in the Department of Statistics at Suzhou University of Science and Technology, I teach courses in optimization theory, operations research, and mathematical modeling. My research focuses on numerical methods for nonlinear and conic optimization, particularly sequential quadratic programming (SQP) methods, optimization with least constraint violation, and primal–dual interior-point methods for nonsymmetric conic optimization. I collaborate with researchers at the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science, Chinese Academy of Sciences, on algorithm design, numerical implementation, and convergence analysis.
